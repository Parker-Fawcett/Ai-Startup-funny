# Fix Validation Against Market (live research, Aug 22 2026)
*Each proposed fix from WHY_NOT_ENOUGH.md tested against buyer behavior, payment-mix data, feature-gate evidence, and category norms. Verdicts: VALIDATED / REFINED / CONDITIONAL.*

| # | Fix | Verdict | Market evidence | Required refinement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Invoice + payment link | **VALIDATED (refined)** | Same-day invoicing = **31% faster payment**, next-day billers lose **12% of invoices to disputes** (septicmind.com). Digital text/email invoices paid **7× faster** than mail; Jobber survey: online-pay contractors collect in **4 days vs 21 days** check-only (fieldservicetools.com, protradehq.com). Payment mix: **44.1% card / 35.5% check / 15.6% cash** (EverCommerce via fieldserv.ai); one NC pumper: only **15–20% of customers pay plastic** but he does it "rather than sending a bill out every month for six months" (Pumper mag). Card acceptance lifts conversions 10–15%. | Don't build Stripe-first. Build **invoice-from-job + pay link + mark-paid(cash/check)**. Checks stay first-class (35.5% prefer; 75% of 65+ write them). Invoices need **line items**: base rate, trip fee ($35–55, 85–95% attach), disposal fee ($40–60), overage per gallon — i.e., a **simple tank-size rate card**, not gallons × price (pulserevops fee benchmarks; Kruser live rate card). Commercial = net-15/30 invoice delivery, never field-pay expectation (septicmind AR guide). |

Until shipped: reword compare pages to match reality. Never demo in a dead zone before then. |

| 4 | Due-soon email/SMS reminders | **VALIDATED — highest ROI** | SMS **98% open / 20% email**, read in **~90 s–3 min**; reminders cut **no-shows 38–50%**; payment reminder texts halve collection time; **seasonal rebooking texts fill slow weeks** — directly monetizing the 3–5 yr pump cycle (textus.com, schedulingkit.com, messageiq.io, fieldserv.ai). ## Cross-cutting validations

- **Free trial is category table stakes** (new finding): HCP/Jobber 14-day, PumpDocket 30-day trials, Roll-Off Amigo free tier — "demo-only" is a documented ServiceCore complaint. Our admin-login-only app is below norm. Ship a read-only seeded trial account. (Adds to fix queue, ~half day.)
- **Same-day-invoice framing beats feature lists in this trade**: every source converges on "invoice before the truck leaves the driveway" as the emotional demo moment (swivl.tech, protradehq, pumper.com). That's the demo script.
- **Kill-switch survives validation**: nothing found suggesting buyers will pay a premium for compliance alone at 2–3× rival prices. Compliance is the *retention* moat and price-defense layer, not the acquisition wedge. Acquisition wedge = close-your-day loop + reminders.

## Revised build order (profit-ranked, evidence-weighted)

1. **Invoice-from-job + rate card + pay-link + mark-paid** (fix 1, refined) — the demo moment, unblocks $299 anchor
2. **Email due-soon reminders** (fix 4) — cheapest churn lever, zero compliance risk
3. **QB/bookkeeper CSV export** (fix 3) — removes bookkeeper veto
4. **Offline honesty: reword pages now; PWA queue for complete-form** (fix 2) — integrity first, then capability
5. **Form 4 fields + photos on complete** (fix 6) — feeds compliance output too
6. **Trial account + self-serve signup** (new) — category norm
7. **$299/$499 re-anchor + annual prepay** (fix 5) — only after 1–4
8. **Driver-per-day + monthly no-pumping notice generator** (fixes 7+8) — cheap differentiators
