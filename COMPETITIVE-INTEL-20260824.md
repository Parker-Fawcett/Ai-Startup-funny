## 3. What this means for the $199 founding offer

**The sub-$200 band is now a five-player bloodbath** (QuoteIQ, SepticMind, PumpDocket, SepticCycle, Tank Track single-truck). We cannot and should not fight there on price.

**What survived the refresh intact — our actual differentiation:**
1. **MA statutory filing depth.** PumpDocket has an "enhanced MA layout" (a trip-ticket template). Nobody else tracks the **14-day 310 CMR 15.351 filing clock per job with a receipt log and town-correct form variants**. That is still ours alone, and it is the thing a MA Board of Health actually enforces.
2. **The route-sale ledger.** Parcel-keyed tank history + one-click diligence ZIP. No rival has this framing or feature.
3. **Flat $199 with zero per-truck math.** Beats Tank Track at 2+ trucks ($274+), beats ServiceCore everywhere, loses to PumpDocket/SepticMind on sticker — which is why the pitch leads with compliance, not price.

**Talk-track updates for the outreach drafts (apply immediately):**
- When they mention PumpDocket's price: *"Their $69 intro is real, and for a trip-ticket generator it's fine. We're the ones tracking your 14-day filing deadline per job, per town, with a receipt log — ask them who files your Form 4 for you."*
- When they mention SepticMind's $79: *"Call them and ask what the price actually is — their own site can't decide between $79 and $499. Then ask them about Massachusetts Title 5 specifically."*
- When they mention Tank Track: *"They just cut prices and shipped an app with zero reviews. Ask them for the 14-day Massachusetts filing feature on that demo call."*
- Never lead with price. Lead with: *"What happens if a Form 4 doesn't get to your Board of Health in 14 days?"* Then show the filing clock on the demo.

**Pricing posture:** hold the $199 founding rate. It is still 2× the bloodbath floor, but the founding framing (locked forever + testimonial trade) is the honest reason, and the compliance depth is the defensible reason. Dropping to $99 makes us invisible in a crowd of $79-$99 players and destroys the revenue line in every model.
