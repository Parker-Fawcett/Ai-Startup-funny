Where PumpRun wins

  • Flat from truck 4: $630 → $499 at four trucks, $780 → $499 at five, unlimited users included either way.
  • Rate lock: lock $499 while per-truck quotes just rose ~20%.
  • Statutory due dates: every tank carries its legal next-due date and an auto-filled, board-formatted pump-out PDF for the 14-day Massachusetts filing clock. Tank Track's manifest coverage is a TX/OH patchwork — a promised Florida integration was publicly missed by its own customers' account.
  • Self-serve CSV import: your route book loads in minutes, day one.
  • Same-day invoicing: every completed stop mints an invoice with a shareable pay link and one-tap check/cash settlement; Tank Track bills per truck but doesn't close the money loop.
  • Email reminders: due-soon and overdue customers get automatic nudges on schedule — the 3-year cycle is won at reminder time.
  • Offline closeout: no signal at the tank? The completed stop saves on the phone — photo included — and sends itself when bars return. Tank Track is browser-only with no offline story.
